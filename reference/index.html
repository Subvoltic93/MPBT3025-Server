These local reference copies support the emulator's gameplay and protocol research:

  • Multiplayer BattleTech 3025 Manual — player-facing rules and interface behavior.
  • Software Design Specification v1.1a — system and gameplay requirements, including Solaris and campaign behavior.

The documents are kept in their original formats. Research notes should identify whether a claim comes from a manual/SDS passage, a packet trace, or a live client observation. These reference copies are project research material; the project does not grant redistribution rights.
