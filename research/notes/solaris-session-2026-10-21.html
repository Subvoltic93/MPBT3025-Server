Correction from the next-session audit: the earlier read of the switch table was wrong. Virtual method 0x4DE4A0 is the lobby connection's inbound game-PDU dispatcher; its table routes 0x8B to handler 0x4DE7D5, 0x8E to 0x4DE8B7, and 0x8F to 0x4DEA0D. Resume update — receive-table correction, 13:48 local

The prior claim that dispatcher 0x4DE4A0 routes wire IDs 0x8B and 0x8E to 0x4DE7D5 and 0x4DE8B7 is incorrect. Its switch computes ID - 0x86; the byte table sends both 0x8B and 0x8E to default 0x4DEF64. The named targets correspond to other entries (0x4DE7D5 is table ID 0x95, 0x4DE8B7 is 0x98, and 0x4DEA0D is 0x99). The 0x99 ready-room handler mapping is confirmed.
